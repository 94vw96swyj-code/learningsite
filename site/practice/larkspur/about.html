About Larkspur Health

Larkspur Health is a nonprofit health system. Our doctors, nurses and staff care for people across the Twin Cities, at every stage of life.

Our mission

Good care, close to home, for everyone who comes through our doors.

A group of nurses opened our first small hospital in Minneapolis in 1958. Today Larkspur Health has 3 hospitals and 24 clinics, and we still put patients first.

Why work here

  • Free classes

    Take up to 6 credits each term at no cost, and get a discount for your family.

  • Health coverage

    Health, dental and vision plans for full-time staff and their families.

  • Retirement

    A pension or retirement plan, with the university adding money every paycheck.

  • Time off

    Up to 4 weeks of vacation in your first years, plus 11 paid holidays.

  • Room to grow

    Free training, mentoring and a clear path to move up.

  • Close to home

    Jobs in St. Paul, Eveleth, Willmar and online across Minnesota.

Benefits

  • Medical, dental and vision insurance
  • Paid vacation, sick time and holidays
  • Retirement plans
  • Life and disability insurance
  • Tuition benefit for you and your family
  • Free bus pass on the Twin Rivers campus

Benefits depend on the job. Each job post lists its benefits.

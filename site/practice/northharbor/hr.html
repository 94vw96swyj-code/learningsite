Human Resources hires people for every City department, from the library to the police department. We also run pay and benefits for City staff.

Job opportunities

Every open City job is posted on our careers portal. You read the posting and apply online there.

View current openings

Paper applications are not accepted. Need help applying? Call us or visit City Hall.

How City hiring works

  1. Find a job on the careers portal and read the whole posting.
  2. Apply online before the closing date. Late applications are not accepted.
  3. We check the minimum qualifications and score your answers to the supplemental questions.
  4. The top-scoring applicants are invited to an interview.

Benefits

Full-time City jobs come with:

  • Health and dental insurance
  • A pension plan for retirement
  • Paid vacation and sick time
  • 11 paid holidays a year
  • Help paying for college classes

Part-time and seasonal jobs have fewer benefits. Each job posting lists its benefits.

Already work for the City? Use Employee login to see your pay stubs and benefits.

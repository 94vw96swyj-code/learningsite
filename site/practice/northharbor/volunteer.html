Volunteer with the City

Give your time to make North Harbor a better place. Meet your neighbors, learn new skills and help your city.

Volunteer work is unpaid.

Ways to help

  • Park cleanup days

    Saturday mornings in spring and fall. We bring the gloves and bags.

  • Library reading buddies

    Read with kids after school, one hour a week.

  • Senior center lunch helpers

    Serve lunch on weekdays, 11:00 am to 1:00 pm.

  • Harbor Days festival crew

    Help with booths and cleanup at the summer festival.

Sign up

Fill out a short volunteer form. A volunteer coordinator will call you within two weeks.

Questions?

Call the volunteer coordinator at (218) 555-0163, or email volunteer@example.com.

Practice job search

How this practice works

  1. Pick a job below and click View job. Read the whole post, then click its Apply button. Each job uses a different kind of online application, like the ones you will see when you apply for real.
  2. Apply with a practice résumé. Each application gives you three practice résumés to choose from. The site fills in the form from the résumé, the way real sites do.
  3. Check every box against the résumé. Some answers will be wrong or missing. Open the résumé, compare, and fix them.
  4. Finish the application. At the end you get a completion code. If your class asks for it, copy the code into your course assignment.

Hourly or salaried. The first five jobs pay by the hour. The last three are salaried jobs. They take longer and need more careful work, like picking the right cover letter.

Every employer here is made up. Use the Back to job board button at the top of any application to come back here and try another one.
